# AMAZONFEE entries: accounting purpose unverified

The source contains entries labelled AMAZON FEE throughout the observed year, with quantities of +1 or -1 and missing customer IDs. Local source inspection also found offsetting amounts, including +GBP 219.76 and -GBP 219.76 five minutes apart on 28 March 2011. This suggests a correction or reversal without establishing its reason. The full-year inspection has not been independently exported from Athena; the two December entries are present in the supplied Athena detail evidence.

The UCI documentation defines C-prefixed invoices as cancellations but does not explain AMAZONFEE or link these entries to promotions. Keep them separate from merchandise returns and describe them as fee-labelled entries of unverified accounting purpose. Negative values do not establish cash refunds, who received money, or whether money moved.

To resolve the meaning, obtain the retailer's ledger mapping or Amazon settlement statements. Commission charges, corrections and promotional adjustments are possible explanations, not confirmed findings.
